Talk about a hygiene or cleanliness situation that you would find particularly unpleasant or disgusting. For example, discovering bugs in a kitchen or bedroom, seeing residue on supposedly clean dishes, finding a shared kitchen covered in old food or oil, entering a very dirty bathroom, finding overflowing garbage, noticing spoiled food smell, or discovering that a place that should be clean has not been cleaned for a long time. Discuss what would bother you most and how you would react.
