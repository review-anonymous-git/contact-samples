# emo-361: Under-Response

Talk about a situation where you have to perform well in front of other people and do not know how it will go. For example, a job interview, an academic or professional interview, giving a presentation, speaking publicly, answering unexpected questions, or waiting before an evaluation. Discuss which part would make you most nervous. The situation can be hypothetical, and you should not name a real employer, school, interviewer, or identifying detail.

## Target Behavior

The topic speaker should discuss the assigned topic naturally. The manipulated responder should keep their words relevant and cooperative, but deliver them with unusually low emotional engagement: restrained, flat, unenthusiastic, or detached.
