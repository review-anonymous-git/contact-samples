# emo-362: Disproportionate Emotional Overreaction

Talk about an ordinary experience that unexpectedly turned out much better than you expected. For example, you might discuss trying a restaurant or food that was surprisingly good, visiting a place that was more enjoyable than expected, joining an activity you did not expect to like, attending an event that turned out to be fun, or receiving a small unexpected benefit or piece of good news. Discuss what happened, what surprised you, and why it made you feel happy.

## Target Behavior

The topic speaker should keep the situation ordinary and low-stakes. The manipulated responder should make reasonable conversational points, but react with noticeably excessive intensity for the situation.
