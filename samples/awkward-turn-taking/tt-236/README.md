# tt-236: Premature Entry

One speaker explains how to troubleshoot something that is not working, such as a phone, computer, appliance, travel plan, or schedule problem. Walk through the steps and why each step would come next.

## Target Behavior

The manipulated listener should begin asking a question or responding slightly before the explainer has finished a step, roughly once every 1-2 turns when it feels natural.
