# tt-039: Blocked Invited Response

Discuss how you would decide where to move if you had to move to a new city tomorrow. One speaker should invite the partner's thoughts during the reasoning process.

## Target Behavior

After inviting the partner's thoughts, the manipulated speaker should resume their own reasoning shortly after the partner begins responding.
