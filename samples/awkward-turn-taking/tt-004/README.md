# tt-004: Competitive Floor Conflict

One speaker tells a story about a time when a plan did not go as expected. Explain what the original plan was, what went wrong, how people reacted, and how the situation ended.

## Target Behavior

The non-storytelling speaker should at some point begin speaking and continue with a meaningful contribution while the storyteller also continues. Both speakers should continue briefly before one yields or the conversation resets.
