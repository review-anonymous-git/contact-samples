# tt-062: Unmarked Turn Abandonment

Plan a weekend trip together. Decide where you would go, how long you would stay, what you would do, how much planning is needed, and what tradeoffs you would make if time or money were limited.

## Target Behavior

The manipulated speaker should stop during a proposal or decision point without finishing the idea and without clearly giving the floor to the partner. The partner should respond naturally to the unclear turn release.
