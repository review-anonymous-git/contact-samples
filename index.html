P: participant · S: supervisor. H-H scores are individual, with video positions and disruption-instruction roles. Natural: both uninstructed; competitive floor conflict: both instructed. For unilateral H-H conditions, Combined averages uninstructed P and S; for natural conversations, P is both participants' mean. Competitive floor conflict uses both participants' mean with S for display only and is excluded from the role-resolved uninstructed-participant MOS sensitivity analysis. H-AI Combined is the mean of P and S. Scores are 1-5 and apply to the full recording.

Display copies: the two audio channels are independently level-normalized and video backgrounds are matted onto light gray using Robust Video Matting; chair and edge remnants may remain. Ratings refer to the original recordings. These copies are not inputs to the paper's audio or timing analyses. Faces and voices remain identifiable. ClearVoice (MossFormer2_SE_48K) speech enhancement is applied to all 18 examples: both human channels in H-H, and only the human channel in H-AI. Channel loudness is matched after enhancement. Enhancement may alter quiet vocalizations; the original media release is retained.

Prompt and event

Talk about a situation where something you had been looking forward to was unexpectedly cancelled or could no longer happen. For example, a trip being cancelled, an event no longer happening, friends changing plans at the last minute, bad weather ruining an outdoor activity, or discovering that something you had anticipated was unavailable. Discuss why you were looking forward to it and how you would feel when the plan fell through. A hypothetical example is fine.

Target behavior. Both speakers should respond naturally. They do not need to exaggerate emotion; they should express the level of affect that would normally arise while discussing the topic.
